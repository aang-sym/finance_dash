Why it's different

Most chip apps are a calculator with a felt background.

The maths was never the hard part. The thing you lose when the chips go away is the weight of them — the clatter, the riffle, the shove. That's what this is for.

One tap, one chip

Tap a stack and a chip lifts off it, with a tick you feel and a clink that rises in pitch as the pile grows. No number pad. No slider. The count is the bet.

A pot you lean over

Tap the pot and the camera moves into it — chips grouped by who put them in, side pots as rings you can read at a glance. Not a receipt. The actual table.

Payouts that land

Winning a $4 pot is a handful of clinks. Winning an $80 one is a four-second avalanche that only your phone hears. The reward scales with the number.
